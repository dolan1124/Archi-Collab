Task Assignment and Follow-up Service ()
A Task Assignment and Follow-up Service is an operational management tool that assigns specific clinical or administrative tasks to healthcare team members and automatically tracks their completion status over time. Its purpose is to establish clear accountability across multidisciplinary teams and ensure that no critical step in a patient's care plan or transition pathway is delayed or forgotten. Example: The service automatically assigns a post-discharge medication reconciliation task to a clinical pharmacist and generates an automated follow-up reminder if the task remains unfulfilled 24 hours later.
